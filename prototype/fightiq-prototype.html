FightIQ · Interactive prototype

You train hard.
You forget most of it.

You leave the gym with three corrections in your head. By Thursday you remember one, and it is usually not the important one. FightIQ remembers all of it and gives you one thing to fix.

TALK, DO NOT TYPE

Two minutes in the changing room

Say what happened. FightIQ pulls out the detail worth keeping and asks one question when it needs to.

IT REMEMBERS

Your coach said it three weeks ago

The same problem keeps turning up in your notes. FightIQ is the one thing in the gym that never forgets it did.

ONE THING TONIGHT

Turn the support foot, then commit

Not a training plan. One cue, built from your own sessions, short enough to hold onto for two hours.

WHEN WORDS ARE NOT ENOUGH

Watch the movement, from any angle

Some corrections cannot be written down. Scrub the movement frame by frame, or open real footage of a fighter doing it properly.

The loop

  1. Log it. Talk for two minutes about what happened. No forms.
  2. Read it back. FightIQ finds the thread and asks one question if the note is ambiguous.
  3. See it. If the fix is hard to picture, watch the movement instead of reading about it.
  4. Zoom out. Every few sessions it shows you what keeps coming up, and what quietly stopped.

And it knows when to stop

Write "got rocked, still foggy" and FightIQ stops coaching.

It reads that from your own words, before any AI runs, so it still works when the network does not. It will not hand you a sparring drill two days later. Instead it opens a stepwise return to training: rest, light movement, solo work, full training without contact, and only then contact, with a minimum of twenty four hours at each step.

No app clears anyone for contact. This one holds that last step shut until you tell it a doctor has seen you, says plainly that it is not a doctor, and gives you a way out when it has read you wrong.

The demo
